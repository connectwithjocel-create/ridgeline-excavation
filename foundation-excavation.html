Overview

What foundation excavation involves.

The foundation hole sets the stage for everything above it. Excavation follows the plan's footprint, depth and over-dig so the foundation crew can work safely and accurately.

Every property is different. The right approach depends on the plans, the ground, access and any engineering or permit requirements — which is why the work starts with understanding the site.

Sitework considerations

What shapes the work.

Soil report

Engineering recommendations often govern depth, over-excavation and bearing conditions.

Rock

Hard rock can change methods, equipment and schedule.

Excavation safety

Open excavations need sloping, benching or protective systems as required.

Spoils

Excavated material needs a place to go — on site for backfill or hauled away.

Typical applications

Where it applies.

Common situations where foundation excavation is part of the project.

APP-01

Full basements

Excavation for full-depth basements on new homes.

APP-02

Crawl spaces & garden levels

Partial-depth excavation, including walkout and garden-level designs on sloped lots.

APP-03

Footings & stem walls

Footing excavation for slab-on-grade and stem wall foundations.

APP-04

Additions

Tying a new foundation into an existing structure with care around the original.

Planning requirements

Before the first cut.

Having these in hand keeps the project moving and helps produce a more accurate estimate.

  • Stamped foundation and site plans
  • Geotechnical / soils report where required
  • Staked footprint and reference elevations
  • Utility locates via Colorado 811 and permits in place

General workflow

STEP 01

Verify layout

Confirm stakes, depths and over-dig against the plans.

STEP 02

Excavate

Dig to depth with planned slopes and working room.

STEP 03

Check bottom

Prepare the bottom for inspection or engineer review where required.

STEP 04

Backfill later

Return after foundation work for backfill and rough grade, as scoped.

FAQ

Foundations questions.

Request an Estimate
Who determines how deep the foundation excavation goes?

The foundation plans and engineering, often informed by a soils report. The excavator follows those documents.

What if rock is found during excavation?

Rock can require different equipment or methods and can affect cost and schedule. How it's handled is agreed between the owner, builder and contractor.

Is backfill included?

It can be, depending on the scope. Backfill usually happens after the foundation is complete and approved.
