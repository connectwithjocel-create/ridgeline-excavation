01

Stage 1

Project Inquiry

Submit the site location and project information.

  • Property location and access
  • What you're planning to build or improve
  • Plans, drawings or photos if available

Photos of the site, a plat or site plan, and your target timeline make the first conversation far more useful.

02

Stage 2

Site Evaluation

Review the property and discuss relevant conditions.

  • Walk the site and note slope, soil and drainage
  • Discuss access, utilities and constraints
  • Identify what engineering or permits may require

A site evaluation is an on-the-ground look — it does not replace a survey, soils report or engineering.

03

Stage 3

Scope & Planning

Identify the proposed work and coordination requirements.

  • Define the scope against the plans
  • Sequence work with other trades
  • Confirm utility locates and permits

Utility locates through Colorado 811 are required before excavation begins.

04

Stage 4

Sitework Execution

Excavation and grading fitted into the construction project.

  • Clearing, excavation and grading as scoped
  • Ongoing grade and safety checks
  • Communication as conditions are uncovered

Unexpected conditions — rock, groundwater, buried debris — are communicated when found, along with options.

05

Stage 5

Project Review

Check completed work against the approved scope.

  • Walk the finished work
  • Confirm grades and site condition
  • Hand off to the next trade

Review confirms the work against the agreed scope; inspections are handled per the permit and plans.
